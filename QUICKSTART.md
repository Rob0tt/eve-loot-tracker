# Quick Start Guide

Get tracking ISK in 30 seconds.

## Installation

1. **Download** `eve_loot_tracker.html`
2. **Open** it in any web browser
3. **Done!** No installation, no setup

## Add Your First Kill

1. Go to [zkillboard.com](https://zkillboard.com)
2. Find a kill you want to track
3. Copy the kill ID from the URL (the number after `/kill/`)
4. Paste it into the tracker
5. Click **Add Kill**
6. Watch the ISK total update! 💰

## Calculate Fleet Splits

1. Add all your kills
2. Look at the right panel
3. Change **Fleet Size** to your pilot count
4. Done! It shows ISK per pilot

## Track Losses

1. Click the **Losses** tab
2. Enter the ship you lost
3. Enter the ISK value
4. Your profit automatically adjusts

## See What Dropped

1. Click the **Loot Breakdown** tab
2. See the top 50 items looted
3. Find out what's most valuable

## Export for Your Fleet

1. Click **Export**
2. Download the text file
3. Share with your fleet for payments
